3 · Three excerpts from the book

For each one: which chapter and figure, what the figure says, and what it means to me.

Excerpt 1 · Textbook Fig. 3.5

My explanation

Potential density cannot be read off temperature or salinity alone: on the same T-S diagram, the isopycnals shift when the reference pressure changes from 0 dbar to 4000 dbar. Parcel 1 has the same density as surface water; the two parcels labelled 2 are Mediterranean (saltier) and Nordic Seas (fresher) source waters — very different salinity, yet comparable density.
Excerpt 2 · Textbook Fig. 5.5

My explanation

Of the 100 units of incoming shortwave radiation, part is absorbed by the atmosphere and part is reflected back to space, leaving about 48 units reaching the surface; the ocean returns energy as longwave radiation, sensible heat and latent heat. This is exactly the surface-flux term of the mixed-layer heat budget.
Excerpt 3 · Textbook Fig. 7.13

My explanation

Wind-stress curl drives surface convergence and divergence, which stretches or squashes the water column; conservation of potential vorticity then pushes the column poleward or equatorward, producing the interior Sverdrup flow, closed by a western boundary current.
