About this site · how it was produced and where the data come from

The page structure, layout, English translation and the story video were produced with an AI assistant (DeepSeek). The course content comes from my own class notes and the lecturer's slides; textbook figures are from Descriptive Physical Oceanography (6th ed.); the ocean data come from NASA MUR SST (JPL/PO.DAAC, via NOAA CoastWatch ERDDAP).

The AI-generated parts have been checked item by item; any remaining errors are mine.
